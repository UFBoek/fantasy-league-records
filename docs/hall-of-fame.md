# FIG Hall of Fame: Smoothed Legacy Points 2.1

## Eligibility

Hall of Fame eligibility requires **500 career Legacy Points** for QB, RB, WR, and TE. This is a complete replacement for the former three-of-five requirements. Eligibility grants consideration, not automatic induction. Josh Allen and Puka Nacua remain permanently enshrined as the founding class. The Hayden 2025 enshrined season is unaffected.

Raising the requirement from 450 to 500 offsets the greater career awards generated by smoothing. The research-only 2017-2025 mock projects 29 players at 500 versus 30 players under the earlier *tiered* 450-point model.

## Completed-season PPG awards: continuous interpolation

A single season bonus is calculated from exact, unrounded **All-Time Combined PPG**, including regular season and playoff FIG starts. A player must have at least **eight official FIG starts** in a **completed** season. A current-season preview can appear but does not contribute earned seasonal points.

The table contains **anchors, not discrete award bands**. At or below each positional floor, the season award is zero. Between every pair of anchors, points increase by linear interpolation; the result is rounded to one decimal point. The maximum seasonal award remains 205 points, even if PPG exceeds the highest anchor.

| Season Legacy Points | QB PPG | RB PPG | WR PPG | TE PPG |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **0 (floor)** | **22** | **13** | **13** | **10** |
| 30 | 25 | 16 | 15.5 | 13.5 |
| 50 | 26.5 | 18.5 | 18.5 | 15 |
| 70 | 28.5 | 19.5 | 19.5 | 16 |
| 95 | 29.5 | 22.5 | 21.5 | 17 |
| 120 | 32 | 26 | 25 | 18.5 |
| 145 | 34.5 | 28 | 27.5 | 20.5 |
| 175 | 37.5 | 30 | 30 | 22.5 |
| 205 (cap) | 41 | 33 | 33 | 25 |

The formula between two surrounding anchors is:

`season_points = lower_points + ((actual_ppg - lower_ppg) / (upper_ppg - lower_ppg)) * (upper_points - lower_points)`

Example: George Pickens has 15.457 WR PPG through seven verified 2026 starts. Interpolating from the WR floor (13 PPG, 0 points) to the first anchor (15.5 PPG, 30 points) produces **29.5 pending points**, rather than zero. He still needs a completed season with eight or more starts to earn the award permanently. A WR at 15.49 PPG earns 29.9 points and at 15.5 earns 30, eliminating the old 0-to-30 cliff.

## Individual-game bonuses: unchanged

Each verified FIG started game awards **at most one bonus**, using only the highest scoring band reached. QB performances scoring 20-29.99 earn **zero** game Legacy Points; QBs begin earning game bonuses at 30+. RB, WR, and TE begin at 20+. The five scoring bands remain 20+, 30+, 40+, 50+, and 60+. Bench games do not count.

| Game score | QB | RB | WR | TE |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 20-29.99 | **0** | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| 30-39.99 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 10 |
| 40-49.99 | 7 | 10 | 10 | 18 |
| 50-59.99 | 13 | 15 | 15 | 26 |
| 60+ | 22 | 25 | 25 | 35 |

## Model calibration and historical comparisons

The 2023-2026 Week 7 FIG archive contains 5,238 verified starts. Recalculating completed-season awards with the smoothing curve yields approximately **10,253.7 season points** plus **4,734 individual-game points**. The resulting share is **68.41% season / 31.59% game**. Previously, the tiered Legacy 2.0 formula generated 7,640 season points and 5,085 game points (60.04% / 39.96%). Raising the eligibility threshold restores historical *selectivity*, not the original 60/40 *composition*. The QB 20-29.99 game bonus has been removed; higher QB bands and all RB, WR, TE game awards remain unchanged.

The research-only 2017-2025 mock, based on FIG custom scoring for every recorded NFL appearance, produces:

| Requirement | Tiered Legacy 2.0 | Smoothed Legacy 2.1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 450 points | 30 players | 39 players |
| **500 points** | Not the former criterion | **29 players** |
| 550 points | Not the former criterion | 24 players |

Under the smoothed mock, Mike Evans totals about **600.1** points and Cooper Kupp about **657.1**. They were at 489 and 576 under the previous tiered model. The mock excludes years before 2017, assumes every NFL appearance could have been a FIG start, and certain pre-2021 bonus stats may be incomplete. Its player totals are **not official FIG eligibility**.

In the actual FIG archive, Josh Allen leads at **470.5**, Ja'Marr Chase has **419**, Christian McCaffrey has **378.6**, and Puka Nacua has **375.5**. No player has earned 500 as of the validated 2026 Week 7 snapshot. These figures change when published FIG game data and completed seasons update.

## Watchlist and leaderboard

A compact **Hall of Fame / Legacy Points** navigation menu appears directly below the Hall masthead on all Hall pages, linking the founding exhibits and the Legacy Points leaderboard in either direction. The Eligibility & Nomination page at `#/hall-of-fame/eligibility` displays the commissioner's curated 35-player watchlist (14 QB, 10 RB, 9 WR, 2 TE) and the searchable full player leaderboard. Josh Allen and Puka Nacua remain enshrined and searchable under All Players, independently of watchlist membership.

The leaderboard shows earned smoothed season points, game points, career total, remaining points to 500, in-progress season preview, and year-by-year scoring. The **All Players** tab hides players who have **zero earned career Legacy Points** (210 displayed from 386 historically rostered players at the current 2026 Week 7 snapshot). It does not remove their data, change their future eligibility, or filter the separately curated commissioner watchlist. It derives official scoring from `data/player_game_log.json`, `data/player_seasons.json`, and `data/league_history.json`, using exact unrounded PPG and verifying matching FIG starts. Season previews are not earned until the season is complete. The existing founding class, enshrined season, and watchlist membership are unaffected by the smoothing formula.
