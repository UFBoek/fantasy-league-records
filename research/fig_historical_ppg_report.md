## Interpretation

The scorer sums only the raw stat categories available in each response.
A category absent across a season is flagged. This can mean no qualifying performances or that Sleeper did not track it; verify material gaps before calling an older season strictly score-identical to modern FIG.
A game is counted where Sleeper indicates a played game or produces nonzero fantasy statistics. NFL byes and DNPs are excluded.
2024 and 2025 weekly scores were independently validated against all available FIG starter point records in the league archive (exact matches).
